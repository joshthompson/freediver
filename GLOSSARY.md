# Freediver

A side-scrolling freediving game where Linkosha the corgi explores the ocean, meets characters and completes their quests.

## Characters & speech

**Character**:
A named creature that can speak, such as Wanda the Cow, Manny the Manatee, the Corgi King or Linkosha.
_Avoid_: NPC

**Diver**:
The freediver the player steers. Linkosha swims alongside and fetches things.

**Realm Guide**:
The themed corgi in each Crystal Realm, who explains the realm at the start, may hand over an item, and congratulates you at the end.
_Avoid_: Themed corgi, host

**Dialog**:
A conversation shown in the dialog box at the bottom of the screen, advanced by the player. It can be blocking (gameplay pauses) or non-blocking (gameplay continues), and its lines matter to a quest.
_Avoid_: Conversation, cutscene

**Background Dialog**:
An ambient line spoken by a character in a speech bubble beside them as the player passes by. It needs no input, disappears on its own, and never matters to a quest.
_Avoid_: Bark, chatter, ambient dialog

## Places

**Corgi Kingdom**:
The king's hall beneath the corgi statue, holding the throne, the Plinths and the King's Portal.
_Avoid_: Kingdom (on its own, for any other place)

**Crystal Realm**:
One of the five places reached through the King's Portal, each hiding one Crystal Corgi. They can be done in any order and replayed once done.
_Avoid_: Zone, dimension, kingdom, level (for these five)

**King's Portal**:
The portal behind the throne in the Corgi Kingdom. Swimming onto it asks which Crystal Realm to travel to, and every trip back from a realm arrives through it.

**Entry Portal**:
The portal you arrive through at the start of a Crystal Realm.

**Exit Portal**:
The portal at the end of a Crystal Realm that takes you back to the Corgi Kingdom. A Crystal Corgi only counts once Linkosha carries it through.

## Breath

**Air Pocket**:
A space you stay inside to breathe, refilling oxygen quickly for as long as you're in it.
_Avoid_: Air bubble, bubble

**Air Bubble**:
A pickup you swim into that gives back a fixed amount of oxygen once.
_Avoid_: Air pocket

**Hazard**:
Anything in a Crystal Realm that takes oxygen the moment it touches you, such as an asteroid, space urchin, steam vent or Egg Bullet.

## Items

**Bait**:
Something the Diver drops to draw nearby trigger fish away for a while. It exists only in the Trigger Fish realm, and you always start with 10.

## Eggs

**Fried Egg**:
A collectible floating in the Fried Egg realm. Collecting 100 fills the Egg-o-meter.

**Egg Fish**:
A fish shaped like a fried egg whose kiss heals the Diver.
_Avoid_: Fried egg fish

**Egg-o-meter**:
The meter in the Fried Egg realm that fills as Fried Eggs are collected.

**Scrambled Egg Monster**:
The boss of the Fried Egg realm, who swells with the collected eggs and then fires Egg Bullets.

**Egg Bullet**:
An egg fired by the Scrambled Egg Monster. Each one is a Hazard and shrinks the monster.

## Progress

**Crystal Corgi**:
A glass corgi statue, one per Crystal Realm, each a different colour: Ruby, Yellow, White, Black and Fire. Collecting all five completes the Corgi King's quest.

**Plinth**:
One of the five stands behind the throne where Linkosha places each Crystal Corgi brought back.

**Realm Failure**:
Failing a Crystal Realm, such as by blacking out. You're sent back to the King's Portal and lose that realm's progress.
_Avoid_: Death, game over

**Achievement**:
A milestone the player unlocks, announced by a popup that disappears after a countdown which only runs while gameplay is running.
