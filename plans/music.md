## Realm Music

Each Crystal Realm has its own music, synthesised in the browser (`src/game/freediver/music/songs.ts`). They borrow from the ocean music: muffled, with slow chord changes and small stepwise melodies.

- **Corgosphere**: spacey and floaty
- **Lair of Trigger Fish**: deep, tense and creeping
- **Fried Egg Dimension**: silly and frantic, turning into boss music when the Scrambled Egg Monster rages
- **Depths**: sparse and calm, like a real freediving contest
- **Hell**: a fast race track, with its own theme for the devil boss fight

The Corgi Kingdom, the cave and the credits have their own songs too. The credits bring back the Kingdom's waltz. Multiplayer has no music, so Players can hear each other.
